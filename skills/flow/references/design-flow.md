# Choosing the steps

Use these modules as questions to consider, not mandatory pipelines. Add a new stage whenever the research or brief shows it is useful. A step earns its place by producing a needed output, resolving a meaningful uncertainty, or checking a concrete risk.

## Work backward from the outcome

1. Identify the deliverable and what the user will do with it.
2. Define observable evidence for accepting it.
3. Derive the necessary intermediate outputs and dependencies.
4. Resolve the most costly uncertainty before the work that depends on it.
5. Choose a sequence, branch, independent research tasks, or a bounded critique/repair loop as appropriate.
6. Cut duplicate research and ceremonial reviews. Scale effort to the task.

## Optional modules

| Module | Add it when… | Concrete output or check |
|---|---|---|
| Inspiration and references | Aesthetic direction, creative conventions, or examples will shape the work. | Relevant references and an explanation of what to learn from them; no uncredited copying. |
| Audience and message | Communication must change a particular audience's understanding or action. | Audience need, central message, and intended action tied to the brief. |
| Copywriting and voice review | Prose will be published, sent, or spoken. | Clear, specific copy matching supplied voice; supported claims and readable pacing. |
| Visual direction | Design decisions must be consistent across assets. | A justified palette, typography, layout, and treatment appropriate to the use. |
| Variants and selection | Several plausible concepts, hooks, designs, names, or takes need comparison. | Meaningfully different candidates assessed against explicit criteria; a selected direction. |
| Storyboard, timing, and takes | Video, animation, or an image sequence needs temporal coherence. | Shot list with purpose, timing, text, motion, sound, generation route, and take-selection criteria. |
| Data collection and reconciliation | Numbers will support the answer. | Sourced data, units and denominators checked, conflicts surfaced, and material calculations reproduced. |
| Options and trade-offs | Strategy, prioritization, or commercial decisions have multiple defensible choices. | Alternatives compared against user-relevant criteria, with assumptions and consequences. |
| Item-level criteria and costings | Business cases, funding requests, or vendor evaluations require defensible detail. | Criteria applied consistently to each item and transparent, dated cost assumptions. |
| Quality rubric | The quality bar needs an explicit operational definition. | Task-specific criteria grounded in relevant expertise; subjective ratings clearly labeled. |
| Prototype or sample | A wrong direction would waste substantial production. | A representative artifact that resolves the risky uncertainty before scaling. |
| Unit-by-unit build | The user wants to co-build a deck, document, or other sequence. | One useful unit at a time with the requested review gates. |
| Pre-mortem or stakeholder perspective | Likely objections could materially change a decision or communication. | Plausible objections and mitigations, explicitly labeled as simulations rather than real views. |
| Independent review | Stakes or complexity justify a separate evaluation and delegation is allowed. | Findings grounded in raw output and the agreed criteria, without a leaked verdict. |
| Distribution and packaging | The brief includes channel variants, exports, or publication. | Requested formats, actual saved state, and publication only within authorization. |

## Match verification to the medium

- **Writing:** trace claims to evidence; check audience fit, voice, structure, and the requested action. A polished draft is not evidence of conversion or engagement.
- **Images:** inspect the actual image, text, required objects, composition, and protected details for edits. Use the required image-generation workflow for raster creation/editing.
- **Video:** verify actual duration, timing, transitions, legibility, motion, sound, and export. Distinguish full playback review from narrower decode/frame checks.
- **Software:** exercise the relevant user journey and inspect affected data/state. Add tests when they resolve concrete risk, not merely to echo the implementation.
- **Analysis:** reproduce important figures, reconcile totals, compare source definitions, and label estimates and unresolved conflicts.
- **Documents/slides:** inspect rendered pages or slides for hierarchy, clipping, omissions, and source support.
- **Physical processes:** distinguish researched instructions from physically tested results; do not claim taste, safety, or performance was tested without evidence.

## Connect mixed tasks

For a campaign with copy and visuals, establish one audience/message brief, then branch into specialist production, then check that the deliverables agree. For a business case plus executive deck, complete the underlying analysis before compressing it into presentation form. Do not run disconnected workflows with conflicting assumptions.

## Stop rules

Stop process research when additional information is unlikely to change the next decision. Revisit the method when evidence contradicts it, a required capability is missing, or repeated failures expose a wrong approach. Stop at explicit user gates. Stop optional review when acceptance criteria pass. Report incomplete outputs honestly.
